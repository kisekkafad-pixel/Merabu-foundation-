What We Do

Programmes built on dignity, not pity

From legal aid to livelihoods, every programme is designed with older persons as active participants and rights-holders, not passive recipients.

Legal Aid & Elder Justice

Free legal clinics, court representation, and rights education ensuring older persons can access justice when their rights are violated.

2,400+ cases resolved 18 clinics nationwide

Health & Wellness

Mobile clinics, health screenings, NCD management, and mental health support tailored to the specific needs of older persons.

25,000+ screenings 45 mobile units

Livelihoods & Economic Security

SAGE advocacy, micro-enterprises, agricultural support, and financial literacy ensuring older persons have sustainable income.

15,000+ elders supported 8 districts

Residential & Home-Based Care

Dignified residential facilities and trained home-based caregivers providing quality care for older persons with high support needs.

500+ in care programmes 3 residential homes

Advocacy & Policy Reform

Parliamentary engagement, policy briefs, and coalition building to reform laws and systems that protect older persons' rights.

12 policies influenced 5 coalitions

Community Engagement

Intergenerational programmes, elder councils, and volunteer networks building age-friendly communities across Uganda.

200+ communities 1,200+ volunteers
